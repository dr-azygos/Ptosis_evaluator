Ptosis Evaluator

AR eyelid measurement using the corneal light reflex

Patient

Setup

The limbus (corneal edge) is found automatically in each eye; this value is the mm size it is scaled to. Adult mean 11.7 mm. Enter the measured white-to-white if known, or use the forehead ruler in Review for a true mm measurement.

How to measure
  1. Seat the patient, head upright, brows relaxed. Hold the phone ~25–30 cm away at the patient's eye level.
  2. Torch turns on automatically. Ask the patient to look straight at the light — a white reflex dot appears on each cornea.
  3. Keep the forehead, both eyes and the nose in the frame — the face finder needs most of the face. If it still can't find one, Capture anyway and drag the markers into place by hand.
  4. Line the eyes up inside the AR guide. When all checks are green the app takes the photo by itself (a short burst; it keeps the frame with the eyes most open, so a blink is never measured). Turn Auto off to use the shutter instead.
  5. The marking is then done on that photo. Measurements are taken along the axis perpendicular to the inter-pupillary line.
  6. In Review, drag any marker to correct it (a loupe appears while dragging; use the arrow pad for fine nudges).
  7. For levator function, capture Down-gaze and Up-gaze while you fix the brow with your thumb.
