How to measure
  1. Seat the patient, head upright, brows relaxed. Hold the phone 25 to 30 cm away at the patient's eye level.
  2. The torch turns on automatically. Ask the patient to look straight at the light. A white reflex dot appears on each cornea.
  3. Keep the forehead, both eyes and the nose in the frame: the face finder needs most of the face. If it still can't find one, press the shutter anyway and drag the markers into place by hand.
  4. Line the eyes up inside the guide. When all checks are green the app takes the photo by itself. It takes a short burst and keeps the frame with the eyes most open, so a blink is never measured. Turn Auto off to use the shutter instead.
  5. The marking is then done on that photo. Distances are measured perpendicular to the line between the pupils.
  6. In Review, pinch to zoom and drag any marker to correct it. A loupe appears while dragging; the arrow pad nudges by about 0.1 mm.
  7. For levator function, capture Down-gaze and Up-gaze while you fix the brow with your thumb.

Accuracy and learning

Agreement with clinical measurements

Learning: each time you save, the app records how far you moved its U, L, C and I markers. After 5 eyes it corrects new captures by the typical amount. Validation: enter your ruler or slit-lamp values in Review before saving. All data stays on this phone.
